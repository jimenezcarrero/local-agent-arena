# pi at a 32K window: the one-token output budget (investigated 2026-10-03)

## Mechanism (pi 0.80.10, the pinned agent; installed package source)

1. Output budget, @earendil-works/pi-ai/dist/api/simple-options.js, clampMaxTokensToContext():
     CONTEXT_SAFETY_TOKENS = 4096; MIN_MAX_TOKENS = 1
     available = model.contextWindow - estimateContextTokens(context) - 4096
     maxTokens = min(model.maxTokens, max(1, available))
   pi sends that as max_completion_tokens (api/openai-completions.js). The estimate is the last
   response's reported usage plus the messages after it (utils/estimate.js).
   At contextWindow 32768 (pi model "local32k"), a conversation of 28,672 tokens or more gets a
   budget of ONE output token, and anything above ~24.6K gets less than 4,096.
2. Compaction, @earendil-works/pi-coding-agent/dist/core/compaction/compaction.js:
     DEFAULT_COMPACTION_SETTINGS = { reserveTokens: 16384, keepRecentTokens: 20000 }
     shouldCompact: contextTokens > contextWindow - reserveTokens   (16,384 at 32K)
   Compaction keeps ~20K tokens of recent messages plus a summary, so at 32K it can leave the
   conversation above the one-token point. Each reply then stops at 'length' after one token,
   and compaction can't cut further: the agent stalls until the session ends.
   65K and 131K windows keep far more headroom and are not affected this way.

## A worked example: J7, j-granite42-3b-vp-r1-a3 (Granite 4.2 3B, vendor, local32k)
From its pi session (tools/pi_length_scan.py --turns <run>):
  turn 4  usage 26,066 -> COMPACTION (tokensBefore 26,290) -> next usage 26,135
  turn 5  a reply stopped at 'length' after 37 tokens (context 28,701) -> COMPACTION (28,701)
          -> next usage 27,468
  turn 6  a reply stopped at 'length' after 473 tokens (28,885): the budget was shrinking
  turn 7  one-token reply (28,487)
  turn 8  a reply cut at 131 tokens, then a one-token reply (28,725)
  turns 9-11  one-token replies (28,774-28,871)
  Turns 7, 9, 10 and 11 are stalled turns (every reply in them was a one-token reply). The
  server's slot held ~28.8K tokens, under its 32,768 window, so llama-server raised no overflow
  error. turns_passed 0/11; how much of that the stall caused is not measured.

# 54 of 161 runs had at least one short length-limited reply; 12 of them had at least one stalled turn.

- 54 runs had at least one short reply: candidate matches, all at local32k.
- 12 of them had at least one stalled turn (7 marathons, 5 32K crushers): demonstrated stalls.
- 3 of those never recovered (ornith10-32k-def3-a3, spark4b-q8-r3-a4-32k,
  spark4b-q8-t03-r3-a4-32k).
- Short replies also occur at a server context under 28,672 (e.g. spark17-q8-a4-32k at 23,657):
  pi budgets from its own estimate, which adds the messages after the last usage report.

## What it means for the Jetson results
- Every 32K session cell used the same pi settings, so the opportunity was the same; the realized
  impact differed by run (most matches recovered; 12 runs had stalled turns).
- The effect on scores is not measured: a stalled turn may or may not have passed otherwise.
- It explains the "turns that end in seconds" of review-J7 (proposal 4) and of the Ornith-1.0
  @32K 7/11 run (stalled turns 10 and 11).
- Not changed for the Jetson history, and not for J8, whose 8B marathon must stay comparable
  with Granite 4.1's 32K run.

## For later tiers (a decision for the campaign, not made here)
Options: run session arenas at >= 65K; or set pi's compaction for small windows (e.g. a smaller
keepRecentTokens and an earlier trigger) so the post-compaction context stays well under
contextWindow - 4096. Either is a benchmark-version change, to be recorded like the arena-3 one.

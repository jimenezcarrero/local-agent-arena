   route x file"), fixed before the remaining cells run. For every remaining V0c cell: 512 and 8K are always
   measured, plus both tool-call gates and memory. If the valid 8K prefill (RESULT line, prompt within +/-20%)

   the cell is recorded "ineligible on speed; 16K/32K not measured" (a FAILFAST line in the speed evidence).

   the memory floor only matters for eligible cells. The cell running when the rule was adopted
   (geniex-cpu-neohorse4b) finishes at full depth under the previous script; its 16K/32K results test the
   rule's assumption. Installed by write-new+mv into run_v0c.sh; v0c_batch.sh itself is unchanged.

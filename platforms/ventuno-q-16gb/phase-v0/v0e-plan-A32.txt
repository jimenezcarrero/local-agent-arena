1. Why, and what changes from A-retry (v0e-plan-A-retry.txt, D136-D139)
  Two changes, each tied to a recorded failure of A-retry's V0e (D139). Nothing else changes.
  (a) -ngl 32 instead of -ngl 99: the 32 blocks stay on the NPU; the output norm and the 248,320-row output
      projection run on the CPU. D155: with the output layer on the NPU, pi's first request got a tool call in 3/20
      replays and pi smoke passed 2/10; with -ngl 32, 20/20 and 10/10 (D6, Fisher p = 2.6e-8 and 7.1e-4). The
      mechanism of the NPU fault is not identified (D156); this plan does not depend on it.
  (b) --cache-ram 5120 instead of 7942: in A-retry's S2 the prompt cache filled to 7.7 GB and MemAvailable fell to
      1.06 GiB (floor 1.5 GiB), about 1.3 GiB for its last hour (monitor export). The shortfall was about 0.45 GiB.
      The cap is cut by 2,822 MiB: the shortfall, plus the output layer's CPU buffers under -ngl 32 (the f32 logits of
      a 512-token ubatch alone are 248,320 x 512 x 4 B = 509 MB; where the NPU path held them is not measured), plus
      about 1.8 GiB of margin. This is an estimate, not a memfloor derivation: the V0e health verdict and memfloor
      --admit 5120 at every step decide. A smaller cache keeps fewer finished prompts for reuse (A-retry held 25);
      one conversation's reuse (S1 item 3) is the slot's own state and is still gated.
  The A-retry evidence for -ngl 99 (V0d AR 16K 318.4/8.40) does not carry over: the prefill path changes.

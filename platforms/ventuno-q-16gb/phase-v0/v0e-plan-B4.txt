1. Why, and what B4 is
  B1d-4B (npu-dev/b1-bonsai/b1d-4b/RESULT.txt) chose Q8 as the only one of three KV options that fits one session at
  32768 and passes parity, T5 and the screening speed bar: one session, q8_0 K and V, flash attention; peak 1,861.5 MiB
  mapped at -c 32768; parity against the board's CPU reference mean KLD 0.000168, top-1 99.39 % (at 1,024 tokens of
  context; that parity window overlapped an agent-run checker, a recorded deviation); T5 20/20; time to first token
  143.3 s at 27,313 tokens with debug logging. Not measured there: agent quality, long-context parity, sustained use.
  V0e is the campaign's qualification for those, unchanged in method; B4 enters it as A32 did (v0e-plan-A32.txt):
  its own admission with the frozen 16K gate, then S1 and S2.
  Same architecture as A32's NeoHorse (qwen35 hybrid, 32 blocks, 8 attention layers, a reasoning chat template), so
  the D171-D175 methods for hybrid cache reuse and reasoning replies apply as they are.

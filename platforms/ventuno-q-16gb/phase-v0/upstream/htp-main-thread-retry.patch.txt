Proposed fix (D127) for the NPU hang, NOT compiled or tested yet: it needs the Hexagon SDK (laptop build of
libggml-htp-v75.so). Applies to 836d57176 and to master f2918cabb (offset 7). Evidence: runs/v0f-hang/v0f-F-1
(fastrpc-journal.txt, 07:57:37: "wait_signal_locked failed with 0x2f" then "dspqueue_peek failed: 0x0000002f").
htp_main_thread used to leave its loop on any dspqueue_peek error other than EWOULDBLOCK/EEXPIRED; after that nothing
reads the session's queue and the host waits forever. The patch retries after 1 ms instead (rate-limited log);
htp_iface_stop still ends the loop through ctx->killed.

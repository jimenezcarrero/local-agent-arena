Proposed amendment to T3a (backend parity, plan section 5) — for review and the owner's decision; nothing changes until
both accept it, and it is settled before any T3a result exists. The thresholds and the matched arms do not change.

Why an amendment is needed
  Section 5 asks for T3a at ubatch 512 and 2048. R1's ledger (../r1-budget/README.txt, budget.txt, budget-clone-all.txt,
  merged in #65 and #66) admits no four-session configuration at ubatch 2048: the compute buffers grow with the ubatch
  (in the first-pass dry run the output session alone needs 1,416 MiB at 2048, mostly logits), NPU-R at 2048 is 934 MiB
  over on its fullest session even before clones (r1-budget/first-pass/budget.txt), and NPU-R' was not computed at
  2048 because it is already over at 1024 under the every-other-session bound. Ubatch 1024 is
  over for NPU-R and within for NPU-R' only under the split-record clone rule (+208.6 MiB), not under the every-other-
  session bound (-160.9 MiB), so the review kept it deferred. So 2048 has no admitted configuration on this board with
  this model, and the case cannot stay as written.

What 2048 was for
  The plan's F9 found that the NPU's logits depart from the CPU's only with multi-token ubatches (full offload; it was
  0.817 mean KLD / 66.0 % top-1). A second, different ubatch size checks that parity holds across the kernels' batch
  tilings and tail handling, not just at one size.

Options (the review and the owner choose one; the proposal's recommendation first). Revised after Codex's review of
0f5f399: 1,024-token chunks divide evenly by 512 and by 256, so a pair of those sizes never runs a remainder (tail)
ubatch; and the former option C (1024 on NPU-R') had no positive margin under the conservative bound (-160.9 MiB), so
it is withdrawn.
  A. (recommended) T3a at ubatch 512 and 384, on the admitted configuration (NPU-R', four sessions), each against the
     matched same-file CPU arms (K4), on the frozen text and token ids of k5-t7/ (the same 20 x 1,024 tokens, so every
     scored position is identical between arms and sizes). With 384 each 1,024-token chunk runs as 384 + 384 + 256: a
     second, different multi-token size and a remainder ubatch in every chunk. Both are admitted by the ledger with
     the same tools and rules (r1-budget/budget.txt, budget-clone-all.txt): 512 at +595.6 MiB on the fullest session
     (+410.9 under the every-other-session bound), 384 at +692.4 (+553.8), board +1,789.8 for 384; and master's own
     Hexagon support code accepts every NPU node at 384 as at 512 (support/s4-rp-ub384.support.txt: 7,752 nodes, 89
     signatures, 0 refused at 8 MiB VTCM; the fail-closed tests 17/17).
  A2. T3a at ubatch 512 and 256: also admitted (256 at +758.3 / +675.8 MiB; support/s4-rp-ub256.support.txt: 0 refused)
     and a different tiling, but with 1,024-token chunks neither size leaves a remainder ubatch: this option does not
     test tail handling, and says so.
  B. T3a at ubatch 512 only. Simplest; loses the second-size and tail checks.
  2048 is removed under every option, with the ledger as the reason; a larger size (1024) may be proposed again only
  with measured R2 peaks that give it a positive conservative margin, and only before any T3a result.
  Under every option: thresholds as frozen (mean KLD <= 0.01, 99th-percentile KLD <= 0.10, top-1 >= 97 %, every value
  finite), never enlarged after NPU results; CPU-A vs CPU-B and a CPU repeat reported as the noise floor; perplexity
  secondary.
